About this project

This dashboard presents a browser-friendly view of the school profile, digital infrastructure, and access-statistics datasets prepared for an analysis of digital education across rural and urban India.

How to interpret these numbers

Values describe records in the project dataset, not a live service or a guarantee of national coverage. “Internet at school” counts records marked Yes for the school internet facility; access and readiness values are averages of the corresponding school-level scores.

Schooling, Connected · An educational data-analysis project Figures are calculated from the project’s school-level CSV files.
