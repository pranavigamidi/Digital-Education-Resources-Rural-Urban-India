CEP-33: Analyze Digital Education Resources and Access Across Rural and Urban India¶

Department of Information Technology¶

CBIT Hyderabad¶

Academic Year: 2026-27¶

Problem Overview¶

This project analyzes digital education resources and access across rural and urban India using multiple educational datasets. The study focuses on internet availability, computer access, ICT facilities, digital infrastructure, and the digital divide between rural and urban schools.

Datasets Used¶

  1. school_profile_final.csv
  2. digital_infrastructure_final.csv
  3. digital_resources_final.csv
  4. access_statistics_final.csv
  5. state_summary_final.csv
  6. rural_urban.csv
  7. internet.csv
  8. computer.csv
  9. schools.csv
  10. UDISE_2022_23_Table_9.9_0.csv
